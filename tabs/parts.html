AGM Batteries: Vehicles with start-stop systems, heavy electrical loads, or OEM AGM batteries must be replaced with AGM. Do not substitute flooded. Always register battery with scan tool on BMW, Mercedes, Audi, and GM vehicles with BCM battery management.

Rotor Minimum Thickness is cast into the rotor hat or edge. Always verify with vehicle-specific data. Replace if at or below minimum, or if insufficient material remains for machining.
Filter Intervals: Engine air — 15,000–30,000 mi or annually. Cabin air — 12,000–15,000 mi or annually (more often in dusty conditions). Oil — per OEM schedule or 5,000–7,500 mi conventional / 10,000–15,000 mi full synthetic.

Serpentine Belt: Inspect for cracks, glazing, fraying, and missing ribs. Replace if cracked or at 60,000–100,000 mi. Always replace tensioner and idler pulleys when replacing belt on high-mileage vehicles.

Timing Belt Failure = Engine Damage. Always flag overdue timing belts as critical. Check water pump condition and replace simultaneously — labor overlap makes it cost-effective.
