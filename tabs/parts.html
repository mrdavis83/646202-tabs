Rotor Minimum Thickness Reference
Minimum thickness is cast into the rotor hat or edge. Always verify with vehicle-specific service manual. Common minimum thicknesses: front rotors typically 20–30mm; rear 9–18mm. Measure at ≥4 points across friction surface. Replace if worn beyond spec OR if insufficient material remains for machining (typical machining removes 0.5–1mm per side).
